# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an item gives a student timely, decision-useful information about a specific law-school or public-service opportunity without overstating eligibility.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Immediate action required.** A named official announcement with an open or upcoming registration window, a near-term deadline, or a material eligibility change for the target path.
- **7-8: High value.** A specific admissions, selection, recruitment, or talent program with reliable process information and enough detail to verify fit.
- **5-6: Planning value.** A policy explanation, historical score information, or future-cycle notice that helps long-term preparation but has no immediate deadline.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Generic summaries, repeated announcements without new details, or commercial exam-preparation content.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumors, screenshots without sources, paid advertising, or claims that a candidate is eligible without evidence.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward a named institution or authority, target cohort, degree or major restrictions, application process, dates, and an official link. Penalize missing dates, unclear jurisdiction, and content that turns an announcement into speculation. Do not score a paid course as an opportunity.

Use three to five specific tags covering opportunity type, region, and application stage.
