# AI foundations profile

Use this profile for content that helps an人工智能大二 beginner build durable foundations in mathematics, Python, Git, Linux, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, networking, machine learning, deep learning, model training, evaluation, experiment design, and research reading.

Typical items include beginner concept explanations, textbook chapters, guided tutorials, worked examples, reproducible notebooks, engineering explainers, evaluation guides, and first-hand lessons about learning or building computer and AI systems.

Prefer material that supports current CS coursework, a small reproducible
exercise, or a capability the student can use in the next one to three months.
The goal is durable understanding and evaluation judgment, not training a
frontier model.

Do not use this profile for product release notes, funding news, opinion without
technical substance, research papers whose prerequisites are too advanced to
explain, long open-ended curricula, or promotional course advertising. A
frontier paper belongs here only when the source itself is primarily teaching a
transferable foundation.
