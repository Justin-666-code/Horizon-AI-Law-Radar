# IP, AI governance and law profile

Use this profile for developments that connect AI or computer technology with intellectual property, copyright, patents, data rights, trade secrets, algorithm governance, platform responsibility, public-sector AI, privacy, or legal practice.

Typical items include enacted rules, drafts, consultations, regulator guidance, enforcement actions, court decisions, official standards, and credible legal analysis tied to a concrete AI or digital-technology issue.

Do not use this profile for generic law-school study material, unrelated criminal cases, general political commentary, marketing for legal services, or policy speculation without a named authority or document. Every selected item must make a clear connection to AI, computer science, or the digital economy.
