# Law and digital governance profile

Use this profile for developments in technology law, digital regulation, data governance, privacy, platform responsibility, intellectual property, competition, liability, evidence, and legal practice affected by AI.

Typical items include enacted rules, drafts, consultations, regulator guidance, enforcement actions, court decisions, official standards, and credible legal analysis tied to a concrete instrument or case.

Do not use this profile for generic political commentary, sensational crime stories, marketing for legal services, or policy speculation without a named authority or document. General legal study material belongs here only when it changes an exam-relevant rule or a technology-governance issue.
