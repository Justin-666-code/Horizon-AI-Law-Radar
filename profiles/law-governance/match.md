# AI, law and public governance profile

Use this profile for developments at one of three independently judged
intersections:

- AI safety governance: evaluation validity, guardrails, accountability,
  incident response, model/system obligations, privacy, or platform
  responsibility.
- Intellectual property: copyright, patents, trademarks, data rights, trade
  secrets, ownership, substantial similarity, licensing, transactions, or
  valuation.
- Digital government: public-sector AI, procurement, administrative
  decision-making, courts and legal technology, or evidence standards for public
  decisions.

Typical items include enacted rules, drafts, consultations, regulator guidance, enforcement actions, court decisions, official standards, digital-government projects, public-sector AI pilots, procurement rules, and credible analysis tied to a concrete AI or digital-technology issue.

Do not use this profile for law-school admissions, targeted-selection
recruitment, civil-service exams, generic law-school study material, unrelated
criminal cases, general political commentary, legal-service marketing, or policy
speculation without a named authority or document. Every selected item must make
a clear connection to AI, computer science, digital systems, or the digital
economy. Do not force all three intersections into one item.
