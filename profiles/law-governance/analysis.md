# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an item gives an AI student accurate, timely insight into a legal rule, regulatory action, or governance debate relevant to AI and digital society.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Material legal change or authoritative decision.** A statute, regulation, judicial interpretation, landmark judgment, enforcement decision, or official standard with concrete scope and consequences.
- **7-8: Important development.** A credible draft, consultation, regulator guidance, or well-evidenced legal analysis that could change compliance, rights, research, or public administration.
- **5-6: Useful background.** A clear explainer, incremental case, or policy discussion that improves understanding but does not represent a current binding change.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Commentary without a named source, narrow market commentary, or legal news with little relevance to technology or public policy.
- **0-2: Noise.** Advertising, rumor, copied content, political outrage without evidence, or unsupported legal conclusions.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize the type of legal instrument, issuing authority, jurisdiction, procedural stage, effective date, and concrete scope. Clearly distinguish enacted law, draft text, consultation, proposal, guidance, judgment, and media interpretation. Do not infer that a proposal is law or that one case creates a general rule.

Use three to five specific tags covering jurisdiction, legal domain, and technology topic.
