# AI research profile

Use this profile for AI and machine-learning research that an undergraduate building a research foundation should know about.

Typical items include new models, training or inference methods, evaluation studies, datasets, benchmarks, interpretability, alignment, multimodal systems, agents, retrieval, efficient inference, and research-oriented industry reports.

Do not use this profile for routine product announcements, funding news, marketing posts, opinion pieces without evidence, or general career news. A short industry report belongs here only when it presents methods, measurements, or a concrete technical finding.
