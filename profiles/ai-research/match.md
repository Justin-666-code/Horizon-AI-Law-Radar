# AI research profile

Use this profile for AI and machine-learning research that helps an undergraduate
understand capability boundaries, evaluation validity, maturity, and realistic
product implications.

Typical items include new models, training or inference methods, evaluation studies, datasets, benchmarks, interpretability, alignment, multimodal systems, agents, retrieval, efficient inference, and research-oriented industry reports.

Do not use this profile for routine product announcements, funding news,
marketing posts, opinion pieces without evidence, or general career news. A
short industry report belongs here only when it presents methods, measurements,
or a concrete technical finding. Do not select research merely because it could
lead the student toward training a frontier model.
