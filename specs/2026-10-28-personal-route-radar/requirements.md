# Personal Route-Aware Horizon

## Why

Horizon must filter and explain information against the user's layered capability
stack instead of treating the five directions as parallel career identities.

The authoritative route is the `personal_route` section in
`C:\Users\16339\.codex\AGENTS.md`.

## Observable Behavior

- Computer and AI foundations remain beginner-readable and support current CS
  coursework.
- Frontier AI research is used to judge capability boundaries and maturity; it
  is not treated as an invitation to train frontier models.
- Product engineering is the core output layer. Technical articles and product
  news are preferred when they reveal requirements, evaluation, reliability,
  fallback, cost, integration, or concrete deliverables.
- AI safety governance, intellectual property, and digital government are
  identified separately. Intellectual property must not be collapsed into
  compliance risk.
- A cross-layer connection is shown only when the source supports it. Unrelated
  content is not decorated with career or governance framing.
- Law-school admissions, civil-service recruitment, exam preparation, and
  generic legal news remain excluded.
- Items are concise enough to support a second-year student without displacing
  CS coursework, law-school preview, or technical practice.

## Constraints

- Keep `primary_only` source selection.
- Preserve the existing Horizon pipeline and profile format.
- Do not create a recommendation to train a frontier model.
- Do not add speculative sources or dependencies.
- Do not rewrite the authoritative global route in this project.

## Out of Scope

- Ranking the user's courses or allocating study hours.
- Editing the knowledge base or its unresolved professional-line conflict.
- Changing source licensing, deployment infrastructure, or delivery behavior.
